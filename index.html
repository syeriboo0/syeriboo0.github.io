MindSync

🥉 Bronze Medal · SciTEx 2026

A mood journal with an AI companion

MindSync is an installable web app (PWA) for tracking mental health. Users log their mood, chat with Moodie, an AI companion, and get weekly insights into their emotional patterns.

  • Moodie chatbot powered by the Gemini API, with encrypted conversations
  • Weekly mood insights generated from journal entries
  • Firebase Authentication and Firestore for secure user data
  • Deployed on Cloudflare as an installable PWA
